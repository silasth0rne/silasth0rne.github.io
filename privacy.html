Privacy policy

Silas has one user, its owner, and only accesses that owner's own Google account, with read-only access to Gmail. Data is used only to answer the owner's requests. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models, and is shared only with the service providers needed to answer those requests. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Silas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
